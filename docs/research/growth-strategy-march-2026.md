# Artha Growth Strategy — March 2026

**Date:** March 16, 2026
**Purpose:** Honest assessment of why users aren't paying, what to change, and two strategic directions forward.

---

## Current State

- **Onboarding:** 10/10 — "idea to live company in 3 minutes" is genuinely impressive
- **Ongoing value:** 3/10 — after the dopamine of launch, users are left with a subdomain website nobody visits, AI tasks that don't measurably move their business forward, and a $49/month bill they can't justify
- **Conversion blocker:** The gap between what's promised ("Agents that build and run your company, 24/7") and what's delivered (a landing page, some research docs, and 1 AI task/day)

---

## Why Users Won't Pay $49/Month

### 1. Promise-Delivery Gap
Marketing says "24/7 agents running your company." Reality is ~5 minutes/day of AI work (35 credits = 1 task/day). Users feel misled.

### 2. No Measurable ROI
Users can't point to a single customer acquired, dollar earned, or meeting booked through Artha. Without proof of value, renewal is emotional — and emotions fade fast.

### 3. Credit Stinginess
35 credits/month feels like a taxi meter, not an employee. The psychology is wrong. Users should feel abundance, not scarcity.

### 4. The Subdomain Problem
`mycompany.tryartha.com` screams "not a real business." Even though Pro offers custom domains, the default experience undermines credibility.

### 5. Competitive Alternatives Are Cheaper and Better
Carrd ($19/yr) + Buffer ($6/mo) + ChatGPT (free) = better output, custom domain, more control, for ~$25/year vs $588/year.

### 6. Free Tier Is a Dead End
Free users get 0 credits. They see their company built but can never run a single task. They have no way to experience ongoing value before being asked to pay $49. This is a conversion killer.

### 7. "Company Builder" Is Too Vague
Nobody searches for "AI company builder." The positioning doesn't map to a specific pain point that a specific person has right now.

## Strategic Direction A: Optimize What Exists (Lower Risk)

### Price Changes
| Current | Proposed |
|---------|----------|
| Free: 0 credits | Free: 5 credits (let them taste the value) |
| Pro: $49/mo, 35 credits | Starter: $19/mo, 20 credits |
| — | Pro: $49/mo, 100 credits |
| Credit pack: $25 for 15 | Credit pack: $10 for 10 (lower barrier) |

### Messaging Changes
- Kill "Agents that build and run your company, 24/7"
- Replace with specific, outcome-oriented messaging per niche:
  - Freelancers: "Your AI marketing team — website, outreach, and content on autopilot"
  - Indie hackers: "Validate startup ideas in 3 minutes. Find customers in 3 days."
  - Consultants: "Stop marketing yourself. Let AI handle it."

### Product Changes
1. **Free tier gets 5 credits** — users experience ongoing value before paying
2. **Custom domain on all tiers** (CNAME) — remove the #1 credibility objection
3. **ROI dashboard + weekly email** — "Artha sent 47 emails, got 3 replies, saved you 6 hours"
4. **More credits at every tier** — 100+ for Pro, or unlimited with rate limiting
5. **Niche-specific onboarding flows** — ask "Are you a freelancer, indie hacker, or consultant?" and tailor the entire experience

### Growth Tactics
- Product Hunt launch (the 3-minute demo is perfect for this)
- YouTube/TikTok demos of the onboarding flow (viral potential)
- Indie Hackers build-in-public with real metrics
- Target r/SideProject, r/Entrepreneur, r/freelance with authentic engagement

## Strategic Direction B: Pivot the Positioning (Higher Risk, Higher Reward)

### The Insight
The agent infrastructure (research, email, website, social, lead gen) is powerful. But "build a company from scratch" is the wrong frame. Nobody's company runs on a subdomain.

### The Pivot: "AI Marketing Team for Existing Businesses"
- User connects their **existing** domain, email, Stripe, social accounts
- Artha runs their marketing: outreach, content, lead gen, SEO, analytics
- The user already has something to sell — Artha helps them sell it
- This is a real $49-99/mo value prop because the user has revenue potential

### Why This Works Better
1. **Clear ROI** — user can measure revenue before/after Artha
2. **No credibility problem** — it's their domain, their brand
3. **Higher willingness to pay** — marketing is a known expense businesses budget for
4. **Lower churn** — if Artha is generating leads/sales, cancellation has a measurable cost
5. **Bigger TAM** — every freelancer, consultant, small business owner needs marketing help

### What Changes
- Onboarding asks for existing business details (not "describe your idea")
- Website builder becomes website optimizer (audit + improve existing site)
- Email writer becomes outreach machine (connected to user's real email)
- Research becomes competitive intelligence (ongoing, not one-time)
- Task system focuses on revenue-generating activities only

## Bottom Line

The technology is strong. The margins are excellent (~96%). The onboarding is magical. What's broken is:

1. **Positioning** — too vague, promises too much
2. **Pricing** — too high for unproven value, too stingy on credits
3. **Free-to-paid funnel** — dead end at 0 credits
4. **Ongoing value** — users can't see or measure what they're getting

Fix these four things and the product has a real shot. The code doesn't need to change much — the strategy does.
